One brain for every robot.

Show it the job. It learns the job. Then it runs the job, on whichever robot is there.

Concept render

Three parts, one piece of software.

  • Teach

    A worker guides the arm through the task a few dozen times. There is no code to write and no programmer to wait for.

  • Learn

    The brain is built on open robot models and trained on our own job data. Each job it learns is saved as a skill any of our robots can load.

  • Run

    The software talks to the machine, checks its own work and stops safely when something is wrong. It reports parts done and hours run.

Same hardware, new job: load a different skill.

Every job our robots do makes the brain better at the next one. That is why the work on shop floors and solar sites matters beyond the job itself.
